About

I build privacy protections that keep their guarantees in real implementations and for the people who rely on them.

I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at Tufts University, advised by Prof. Johes Bater. I've also worked with Prof. Mark Hempstead and Prof. Daniel Votipka. I'm a member of the Tufts Security and Privacy Lab. I'm applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

With Prof. Bater, I have a workshop paper on differentially private query processing under submission. With Prof. Bater and Prof. Hempstead, I'm finishing a second DP paper that connects privacy guarantees to the systems and hardware they run on.

Outside academia, I'm a contract backend and data engineer at Chatsi AI, a venture-funded startup. I design secure, usable internal APIs that bring customer data into the company's systems, and I work with the senior AI architect on vector search infrastructure.
